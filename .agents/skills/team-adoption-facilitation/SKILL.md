# Team Adoption Facilitation

Carry the team's intended improvement into everyday work. Start from an existing
work record, not a culture survey or compulsory new form. This Skill designs a
reviewable arrangement; it grants no organizational authority, employment change,
new tool permission or rollout approval.

Read [operating-and-measures.md](references/operating-and-measures.md) for decision
rights, organizational alternatives, measures and difficult questions. Use
`templates/team-adoption-brief.md` only where it helps the existing record.

## Facilitate one introduction

1. **Recover the work and the people affected.** Name the parent work, the next
   real handoff and whose experience should improve. In development, distinguish
   the team's requirements/design/implementation/review from the product user's
   work. Recover conditions, versions, allowed environment and unknowns from the
   existing record. Use existing work-understanding methods if that is unclear.
2. **Hear the local history before diagnosing resistance.** Ask one useful
   question: “What went wrong or helped when this team last changed its way of
   working?” Then hear the implementer, receiver/reviewer and decision owner.
   Return a small account of what they protect, what they fear losing and what
   would make a trial worthwhile. Separate their words, evidence and your
   hypothesis. Treat concerns about errors, workload, skill, evaluation or job
   security as information; do not label a person unwilling or promise job safety
   on someone else's behalf. Validate your account with a concrete recent case.
3. **Separate decisions from actions.** For each changed handoff, name who
   proposes, decides, executes, checks, stops and resumes it, plus the evidence
   and time/capacity they need. AI can propose or perform permitted work; a named
   human/organization owns the applicable decisions. Being “low risk” is not
   permission. Human review needs source access, skill, time and power to reject;
   a button or a confidence score alone supplies none of these. Return missing
   conditions to Definition or work design instead of assigning authority.
4. **Choose a feasible support arrangement.** Compare central, local and shared
   responsibility by actual data/tool constraints, cross-team reuse, decision
   delay, local knowledge and support capacity. Name which decisions are local,
   which use common rules, which exceptions need specialist review, and who
   answers when the usual owner is absent. Do not create a CoE because a diagram
   has one, or choose a structure from employee count alone. Have resource owners
   confirm the proposed capacity; unknown capacity stays an open condition.
5. **Make change usable for each role.** Agree why the change matters and hear
   whether people want to try it. Distinguish missing reason, participation,
   knowledge, practical ability/resources and reinforcement. Match the response:
   explain the work outcome; address the concrete concern; practise; remove access
   or time barriers; or provide continuing support. More training does not fix an
   unavailable reviewer. A sponsor removes actual obstacles and secures resources;
   frontline participants choose useful work and report what does not fit.
6. **Practise the receiving work, not just a demo.** Choose one authorized real
   case or a clearly fictional stand-in. Have the person receive the inputs,
   request a draft, inspect its rationale/sources, explain a decision, correct a
   defect and pass the result on. Include an uncertain or failed case and the
   human fallback. Ask them to do it back without the facilitator filling gaps.
   Record the artifact, observation and remaining help needed. A simulated role
   answer is not that person's consent or evidence of actual competence.
7. **Judge benefit with the whole handoff.** Use `ai-pilot-evaluation` to keep
   quality, meaningful use and effect separate. Include review, correction,
   waiting, learning and maintenance effort, plus whose burden moves elsewhere.
   Define denominator, case mix, window, baseline, observer and unacceptable
   failures before comparing. Treat AI usage, lines generated and hours saved as
   possible observations, not employee performance or realized financial benefit.
   Recognize reasoning, safe correction, useful sharing and work outcomes; route
   proposed personnel policy to its actual decision owners.
8. **Answer the unresolved questions honestly.** For error, demo-to-operation,
   data, economics and responsibility questions, return what is known, evidence,
   limits, the next test and the decision owner. Use the reference's response
   pattern. When evidence or permission is absent, prepare the explanation and
   allowed rehearsal; stop only the dependent action. Do not dismiss risk as
   resistance, export data to prove value, or claim a human check removes liability.
9. **Return a decision and keep learning.** Choose prepare only, bounded trial,
   revise/retest, pause or stop, with reasons. Local work can continue inside its
   established boundary while an expansion question is unresolved. Before actual
   introduction, verify permissions, receiver capacity, support, stop/fallback,
   observation and the authorized start decision. Feed a failed rehearsal into
   the nearest Skill/source/hand-off and retry under different conditions.
   Share safe successes and failures with applicability and unknowns; restrict
   protected content, not inconvenient findings. Review again when participants,
   purpose, workload, source, permission or tool changes.

## Return a usable agreement

Keep purpose and affected work, current/proposed roles, participants' concerns,
confirmed versus proposed boundaries, rehearsal evidence, three-layer measures,
next decision/owner and unresolved conditions together. State what the next
person can actually do now, where they return for help and what remains untested.
Use [the development-team rehearsal](https://github.com/maakbo/fde/blob/main/examples/field-delivery/order-search/team-trial.md)
for a fictional application. Do not present its roles or thresholds as defaults.
